The Cocktail Reel

Pour like you
know what you're doing.

Six cocktails, six bundles, zero bartending school. Silent looping pours — recipes below each pour.

Pouring
— Reel No. 01
The Old Fashioned
Recipe 2 oz bourbon · 1 sugar cube · 2 dashes Angostura bitters · orange peel. Muddle, stir over ice, garnish.
Pairs with the Date Night bundle
Shaking
— Reel No. 02
The Margarita
Recipe 2 oz tequila · 1 oz lime juice · 1 oz triple sec · salt rim. Shake, strain, sip slow.
Pairs with the Sun's Out bundle
Espresso
— Reel No. 03
The Espresso Martini
Recipe 2 oz premium vodka · 1 oz cold espresso · 1 oz coffee liqueur · ½ oz simple syrup. Shake hard, double-strain into chilled coupe. Three coffee beans on the foam.
Pairs with the After Hours bundle
Muddling
— Reel No. 04
The Mojito
Recipe 2 oz white rum · 1 oz lime juice · 2 tsp sugar · 6 mint leaves · soda water. Muddle, build, top.
Pairs with the Party Night bundle
Building
— Reel No. 05
The Gin & Tonic
Recipe 2 oz gin · 4 oz quality tonic · 1 cucumber slice · juniper berries. Build over big ice, stir gently.
Pairs with the Quiet One bundle
Citrus
— Reel No. 06
The Whiskey Sour
Recipe 2 oz bourbon · ¾ oz lemon juice · ¾ oz simple syrup · 1 egg white (optional). Dry shake, then wet shake.
Pairs with the Game Night bundle
